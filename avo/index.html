Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you sign up

What is a Virtual Office from Alliance?

A legitimate commercial address inside a staffed, professional office center, not a mailbox store. Register your business with it, receive concierge-handled mail, and book meeting rooms and day offices on demand through the app, without the cost or commitment of a traditional lease.

What's the difference between a Virtual Office and a PO Box?

A PO Box is a mailing address at a postal or pack-and-ship store. A Virtual Office from Alliance is a real business address inside a staffed office building, a location you can physically use for meetings, mail and business registration.

Why do I need to notarize USPS Form 1583?

Alliance is a USPS-licensed Commercial Mail Receiving Agency, so federal regulations require a notarized Form 1583 plus two forms of government ID on file before any mail can be received or forwarded. Three ways to do it:

  • Complimentary online notary during checkout
  • Download the pre-filled form and use your own local notary
  • Sign in person at a staffed partner center and skip notarization entirely
Can I cancel at any time?

Virtual Office plans start with a 6-month initial term, then renew month to month. Cancel through the online account portal with at least 30 days' notice; the cancellation takes effect on the last day of the following month. Requests by phone or email alone are not accepted.

What happens if I go over my Live Receptionist minutes?

Additional minutes are billed at $1.75 per minute, calculated and invoiced at the end of your billing month on top of your plan fee. There is no overage during your first month of service, and you can change plans at any time.

How do I update my forwarding address or contact details?

Sign in to the portal or app and open Account, or ask us in chat. We verify your name plus the email or phone number on file (or your account number) before any change, and we confirm the current forwarding address back to you before a new one is applied. Changes usually apply within one business day.
